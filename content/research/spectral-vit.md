For my senior thesis in physics and astronomy at the University of Rochester, I developed
**Spectral-ViT**, a vision-transformer model that classifies supernovae from their spectra. The
project adapted the transformer architecture to supernova detection in data from the Dark Energy
Spectroscopic Instrument (**DESI**) survey.
