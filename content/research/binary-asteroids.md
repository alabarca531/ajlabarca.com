As an undergraduate researcher with Prof. Alice Quillen, I studied binary asteroid systems whose
secondary tumbles instead of spinning neatly about its principal axis. That non-principal axis
rotation weakens **BYORP**, the radiation-driven effect that slowly grows or shrinks the orbit of a
binary pair. The work was published in *Icarus* in 2021.
