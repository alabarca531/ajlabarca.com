## Interests

- **Bayesian estimation.** Recursive filtering for state estimation in dynamic, uncertain environments.
- **Data association.** Deciding which measurement belongs to which object when there are many of both.
- **Time series classification.** Machine learning that detects and diagnoses faults in aerospace systems from telemetry.
